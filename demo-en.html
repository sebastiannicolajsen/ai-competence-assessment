Straight talk

You get moving fast and you keep going until the answer holds up — that's clear across the conversations. But you almost never check anything afterwards. You correct phrasing, not substance, and in the cases where a figure or a claim was wrong, it went through uncaught. This isn't about using AI less; it's the one place your workflow is missing a step.

Your next moves
01When an answer looks right, stop and check one thing you can verify — a number, a name, a date — before you use it. That's exactly where over-trust creeps in.
02Once a week, ask for the opposite: "what argues against this?" You'll spot fast where the answer was thin.
